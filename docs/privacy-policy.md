partymate helps people meet others who are going to the same invite-only party. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address, used to sign you in.
- **Profile:** your first name, photos, a short bio, and optionally your Instagram username.
- **Parties:** parties you create or join, their details, and your RSVP.
- **Likes and matches:** who you like or pass on at a party, and your matches.
- **Messages:** chats with your matches, including when they're delivered and read.
- **Reports and blocks:** reports you make about other users, and people you block.
- **Device token:** if you allow notifications, a token so we can send them to your phone.

We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Who can see what

- Your profile is only shown to people going to the same party as you.
- Your Instagram username is only shown to people you've matched with.
- Likes are private. Someone only finds out you liked them if they like you too.
- Messages are only visible to you and the person you're talking to.

## How we use it

Only to run partymate: signing you in, showing parties and guests, creating matches, delivering messages and notifications, and keeping the community safe by reviewing reports. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and hosting provider, [region]. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Leaving a party removes your likes and matches from that party. Reports may be kept for a limited time after an account is deleted, where needed to keep the community safe.

## Deleting your account

Go to Profile > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, the parties you host, your matches, and your messages.

## Age

partymate is for people 18 and older.
